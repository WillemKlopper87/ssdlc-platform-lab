Gate policy

Which findings raise an automated PR, and which block a merge outright. Changing anything here changes the contract digest — every watched repo re-evaluates on its next pipeline, and the bot stops voting until operators set the new expected digest.

Exceptions

Two-party risk acceptance for a genuinely new finding — accepted debt only, never a secret. Neither approver can be the requester, and both must be distinct people even if one holds both roles.
